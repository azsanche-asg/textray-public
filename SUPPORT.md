# Support and feedback

Email [hello@burritoslabs.com](mailto:hello@burritoslabs.com) for help with TextRay, purchases, activation or recovery.

For a reproducible product issue, use this repository’s issue form. Include the TextRay version/build, macOS version, what you expected, what happened and the shortest steps you can reproduce. Say whether the problem involves a saved file, an unsaved draft, a session or a View. A small fictional fixture is usually more useful than a large private document.

Do not post activation/recovery codes, receipts, device proofs, customer text, complete library databases or private screenshots in a public issue. For a security concern, use [the private reporting route](SECURITY.md).

The in-app **Support & Policies** page provides offline access to support and policy information. [Engineering evidence](docs/QUALIFICATION.md) documents the scope of current qualification; it is not a promise of a particular response time.
